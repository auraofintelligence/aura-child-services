FROM THE ORIGINAL PROPOSAL

Project reading and development sources

The references below distinguish the Aura proposals from the independent guidance used for Australian care, privacy, education and research. The preceding narrative brings the proposals together around childhood and the transition into adult life. Suggested benefits remain questions for practical evaluation.

P1 Verse Box Trial Proposal Version 1 Corrected

Luke Nathan Hayes, 19 October 2026. The supported computing station, personal Aura, access to learning tools and proposed trial arrangements. Supplied PDF and associated Personal Aura page.

Open source

P2 Aura Teacher Training 60 Days

Luke Nathan Hayes, 14 October 2026. Supplied PDF, Aura_Teacher_Training_60_Days(3).pdf. Reflection, memory, reasoning, practical functions, simulations, social learning and broader civic and scientific inquiry. Used as an adult facilitator development source; the sixty-day structure is not a child participation requirement.

Luke Nathan Hayes, edition 2, 13 October 2026. Supplied PDF, Aura_Genesis_Protocol_Game_Design_Document(2).pdf. Adult Genesis design, personal reflection and Truth Engine research. The adult design uses HBOT; this proposal uses Verse Box for children and teens.

How the proposal status is expressed

The project papers contain ambitions, design choices and research directions. They are not evidence of established child welfare outcomes, operating institutions or guaranteed future opportunities. Older monetary or token-redemption descriptions in the Olympic papers are superseded here by the current nonmonetary reciprocity definition. Their clinical and technical claims are not used as evidence of benefits for children.

Public guidance was checked on 25 October 2026. Any use in an individual matter needs the actual jurisdiction, operative law, circumstances and responsible professional. A historical collection is useful for understanding origins and change alongside that current work.

PRIMARY GUIDANCE REVIEWED · 28 October 2026

Three current source checks.

These brief checks sit alongside the source document. The remaining reference register preserves the proposal’s source context; it is not a claim that every linked source has been reverified.

OAIC: children and young people ↗

Capacity for a privacy decision is assessed in the circumstances; the guidance does not give one universal decision age. Coverage also differs between settings.

Australian Human Rights Commission: child safe organisations ↗

The National Principles place safety, participation, suitable adults, child-focused complaints and ongoing review within organisational practice.

Queensland: charter of rights for a child in care ↗

Queensland’s charter includes privacy, identity and cultural connection, participation in decisions and support for the transition from care.

Actual authority, duties and approval depend on the organisation, jurisdiction, role and activity.
