Welcome

Begin with something you enjoy. Build understanding, make something of your own and find people who help you take the next step.

A wider opportunity

The proposal connects everyday childhood opportunity with a wider question: how can young people share in the capabilities of increasingly powerful intelligence?

↗03

Aura and self understanding

A supported computing station offers a place to make, learn and reflect. The young person brings the meaning; the record can change as their understanding grows.

↗04

Understanding law

Follow a question to a readable source. Learn why a rule exists, whose decision it affects and what needs checking in the present setting.

↗05

A first project

A small creative project can reveal what someone enjoys, what gets in the way and which person or resource would help them continue.

↗06

Family, care and identity

A life can hold several important relationships. Chosen memories, useful information and cultural connections need room to retain their meaning through change.

↗07

Growing into adult life

Mentors, practical work experience, shared productive ownership and planned time for life offer different possibilities to investigate as young people grow into adulthood.

↗08

Contribution and wellbeing

A useful task, a shared meal or a welcoming invitation can connect people. Communities choose their recognition, while essential support remains independent of contribution.

↗09

2032 and civic futures

Local learning and a lasting volunteer network connect the Olympic horizon with young adults investigating the future of their communities, Earth and space.

↗10

People and personal control

The young person can ask questions, correct a record and turn to accountable adults. Learning, recording, research and publication each need an understandable purpose and actual arrangements.

↗11

Developing the proposal

A useful pilot needs a willing host, time, support and something concrete to try. Young people help shape it, and what happens changes the next step.
